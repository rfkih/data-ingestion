## Reading (written after the run; verdicts fixed before it)

The denser sample answered the question in an unexpected way: on 5-day windows Stockbit's own detector rarely labels anything
— 1,776 of 1,886 windows carry no accumulation/distribution label at all, 53 are "Acc", 57 "Dist" (the label needs a
concentration that 5 days seldom reach). So the arms have 5–56 trades, not the 300 the rule asks for, and what they show is
noise with no direction: "Acc" +0.12 % net at 5 days and −1.50 % at 20 days, the mirror "Dist" +1.18 % at 20 days, the
persistent top-1 buyer negative at every horizon. The +2–5 pp gap seen on 20-day windows in menu 4 does not reappear where
the labels exist here.

**Verdict: 0 of 9. Bandarmologi, as far as this feed can express it (the API's labels, top-broker concentration, investor-class
net flow, persistence), is closed: 71 pre-registered trials over five menus (cumulative 267), none tradeable after costs.
The broker feed keeps one job — the card: who is accumulating or distributing a name the annual book holds is a fact worth
reading, not a signal worth trading.**
