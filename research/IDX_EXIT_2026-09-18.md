## Reading (written after the run; the reading rules above were fixed before it)

**No textbook exit beats the deployed 10 % trailing stop by the declared bar — on either universe.** Fifteen exits from the
trend-following literature (Chandelier, Parabolic SAR, Turtle channel, moving averages, O'Neil 8/20, Van Tharp 3R bracket, scale-outs,
ratchet, climax/RSI take-profits, dead-money stop) were run on the same entries, book and costs; none reached Sharpe + 0.15 with the
drawdown held, so trail10 stays in both trend books.

What the families do (universe `small`, the deployed rule; trail10 = +29.9 %/yr, Sharpe 1.34, mDD −30 %, keeps 25 % of the open profit):
- **Selling into strength destroys the edge.** RSI ≥ 75 (+3.9 %/yr), climax top (+15.7 %), O'Neil's +20 % target (+16.5 %, mDD −42 %):
  the return of this strategy sits in the tail of a few runners; every rule that sells a winner early gives that tail away.
- **Looser trend-break exits earn more and draw down more.** MA20 +35.8 %/yr, MA50 +34.5 %, SAR +34.4 %, Turtle-10 +33.5 %, Chandelier-3
  +32.3 % — all at mDD −39 … −44 % instead of −30 %. They let the best trades run (MFE 28–38 %) and give more back on the way out.
- **Tighter (Chandelier-2) cuts both** (+25.1 %, mDD −27 %, 997 trades of 10 days).
- **Scale-outs smooth the book at a cost in return.** Thirds (⅓ at +10 %, ⅓ at +20 %, trail the rest): +22.8 %/yr, Sharpe 1.32, hit 47 %,
  **mDD −24.6 %** — the only arm in 343 trials to pass the money rule (n 495, t 3.6, Sharpe ≥ 1, mDD ≤ 25 %, 5/7 years, ≥ random + 0.5;
  DSR 0.68). It passes at the boundary and by trading 7 pp of CAGR for 5.6 pp of drawdown; on LIQ it is Sharpe 0.83 / mDD −31 %, so the
  pass is not robust across universes. Recorded as a candidate by the letter of the rule, NOT adopted: it is a risk preference
  (smoother, less), not an edge. Half-at-+20 % sits between (+25.8 %, mDD −27 %).
- **The one exit that moves every number the right way is the fixed-target bracket `tp3r`** (stop at entry − 2 × ATR20, take profit at
  entry + 3R, nothing else): small +33.6 %/yr, Sharpe 1.41, t 4.2, mDD −29.7 % (same), keeps 37 % of the open profit; LIQ +21.9 %/yr,
  Sharpe 1.01, mDD −37 % (1 pp deeper). Consistent direction on both universes, but +0.07 / +0.09 Sharpe is under the declared +0.15 bar
  and could be noise between two exits that share most trades. Not proven better; the only candidate for a follow-up.

**Verdict: 0 of 30 by the adoption rule; trail10 stays. One money-rule pass (`thirds|small`, boundary, single universe) recorded, not
adopted. Follow-up if wanted: a pre-registered neighbourhood of `tp3r` (stop 1.5/2/2.5 × ATR × target 2/3/4 R, both universes) — a
change to the live book only on a robust pass and the operator's decision.**
