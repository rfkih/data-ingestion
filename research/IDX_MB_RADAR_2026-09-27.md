## Reading

1. **As a radar the momentum family works, every year.** A C0 flag touches 2x within a year 30 % of the time against 18 % for a
   random name on the same date (lift 1.44-2.20 in all five years). Stacking the exchange's own attention flag (UMA) or a
   structural event raises the hit rate to 42-45 % - roughly one flagged name in 2.3 becomes a double at some point.
2. **Events alone are not a radar** (F4 1.38, F5 1.06): they matter as CONTEXT for a name that is already moving. That is
   consistent with SX-1's placebo: the event dates carry little timing; the event marks the type of company.
3. **The price of the radar:** about half the flagged names are at some point 30 % below the flag price, the median 12-month
   outcome is -5..-14 %, and only 13-22 % are still 2x at month 12. The radar finds rockets; holding them needs an exit rule,
   and SX-1 / TR-2 / TR-3 showed no exit turns that into a better book than the deployed trend sleeve.
4. Caveats: F6-F8 cover 2023-07 .. 2025-09 only (the announcement archive); 2025 was a small-cap rally (base 29-35 %); the
   flags are correlated (they are not 8 independent findings); N = 1,087.
