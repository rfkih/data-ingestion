# IDX — A ten-name book, and can the weights lean toward the winners? (2026-09-12)

**Verdict.** Ten names can work, but only one way of choosing them holds up across calendars: the ten cheapest names
that also pass the strict quality gate, held at equal weight. It matched or beat the full list in every rebalance calendar
(+105 / +132 / +138 / +96 % vs the whole fifth's +106 / +111 / +114 / +63 %, Sharpe 0.78 to 1.05, drawdown 22 to 26 %).
Leaning the weights toward the top of any ranking added nothing robust: it helped in one or two calendars and hurt in the
others. Momentum picks the eventual winners spectacularly when the rebalance date catches them early (+204 % with a
momentum-tilted ten at May, +194 % at February) and misses them in the other two calendars (+23 % at November). The real
winners of each year sat at median rank 9 of the list, half of them with negative momentum; no feature available on the
day of selection rank-correlates with the forward year at more than 0.18. Nothing here is certified (DSR at N = 32 trials:
0.36 to 0.60). Script `research/idx_top10.py`; outputs `research-scratch/idx-screen/top10_out.txt`, `top10_results.json`.

## Pre-registered menu

From L, the deployed list (loose-gate composite top fifth, 10 to 24 names), ten names by one criterion, weighted one way:

| Selection | Ten names by | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| loose10 | composite rank within L | the rank carries information inside the list |
| strict10 | the strict-gate composite (ROE ≥ 10 %, profit this and prior year, CFO > 0, D/E ≤ 1.5) | quality screens out the traps |
| cheap10 | earnings yield alone within L | deepest value wins |
| mom10 | 12-1 month price momentum within L | value plus momentum: cheap names already recovering |
| growth10 | audited profit growth within L | earnings momentum |

Weights within the ten, in the selection's own order: equal; top five doubled (13.3 % vs 6.7 %); linear rank (18 % down
to 1.8 %). Fifteen portfolios, four rebalance months, the same simulator and costs as rev. 3. Trials counted cumulatively
for the session: 13 (rev. 3) + 4 (book size) + 15 = 32.

Reading:

1. **Quality is the only selection that is robust.** strict10 is within a point of the full list at May and ahead at
   February, August and November, with a higher Sharpe in every calendar. It is the same finding as rev. 3 (the strict
   composite leads) seen from the ten-name side.
2. **Tilting the weights does not pay.** For strict10 the tilt helps at May and hurts at Feb, Aug and Nov. For cheap10 it
   hurts everywhere. For loose10 it is noise. Equal weight is the answer to "how to weight the ten".
3. **Momentum is the winner-catcher, and a coin flip.** mom10 wins May and February by a wide margin because those dates
   caught TRJA, ITMG, MEDC and PTRO on their way up; at August and November the same rule is average to poor, and the
   momentum-tilted version collapses to +23 % at November. That is the shape of "maximal on the real winner": very high
   when the calendar cooperates, worse than the plain rule when it does not.
4. **Deep value alone and profit growth alone are worse than the rule** in three of four calendars.

## What this means for a ten-name book

- **Choose the ten by the strict composite, equal weight.** Today: BBNI, GJTL, SRTG, ASII, CTRA, BMRI, SIMP, PWON, ACES,
  LSIP (SRTG carries a trailing-loss warning; a judgment call, not an automatic exclusion).
- **Do not tilt the weights** toward the top of the ranking; the evidence says it adds dispersion, not return.
- **Momentum as a winner-catcher is real but calendar-dependent.** If the operator wants that profile, it belongs on paper
  first: a second paper book (momentum-ten) alongside the rule, so that May 2027 has an out-of-sample year for both.
- **Expect one name to be 10 % of the book** and a drawdown of 25 to 30 %; a Sritex-type zero costs 10 % in a day.
- None of this is certified. DSR at N = 32: strict10 0.36 (May) to 0.52 (Feb); mom10 tilted up to 0.60. The rule itself
  stays the whole fifth; the strict switch remains the May-2027 decision, and a ten-name book is a sizing choice inside it.

## Addendum, same evening: fifteen names, and the production picker

The menu was re-run through the production module (`strategies.pick`) and reproduced every ten-name number above to the
decimal, then run again at fifteen names (15 more trials; DSR at N = 47). At fifteen, May: strict +146 % (Sharpe 0.98,
drawdown 22 %, identical to the natural strict list, which holds 10 to 15 anyway), rule +98 %, value +122 %, momentum
+110 % (rank-weighted +156 %), growth +129 %. The ordering across calendars is unchanged: strict is the only family that
holds up everywhere; momentum's edge shrinks as the book widens. Both size records are in `idx.strategy_history` and on
the app's Strategies page.
