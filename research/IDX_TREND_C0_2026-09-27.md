## Reading

1. **SX-1's 45.8 %/yr was an engine artefact, not an edge.** On the desk's own engine, C0 (T3) is 15.1 %/0.99 with a -36 %
   drawdown against the deployed rule's 9.3 %/0.90/-18 % - a little more return for twice the drawdown. SX-1's simulator was
   fully invested (20 x 5 %) in every signal of the whole Rp 1 bn universe with no lot sizes or quoted spreads.
2. **Every arm, the deployed one included, is a 2025 story.** No arm has a positive first half (2022-01 .. 2024-06); 2025
   (+65 % REF, +124 % T2) carries all of them. That is the regime the sleeve lives on, and a reason not to widen it.
3. **The long trail hurts on the desk's names** (T1): with a 25 % trail the K-10 book holds its slots through reversals and
   takes a quarter of the trades. The multibaggers' "let it run" is paid for by giving back the trend sleeve's small wins.
4. **The universe widening (T2)** is the only piece with a better rupiah book (17.5 %/1.26), but the K-10 simulator
   disagrees (0.90 vs 1.42, mDD -42 %) and its first half is negative: not a rule, a 2025 bet on small names.

Note: REF's rupiah book here (9.3 %/0.90) is below #192's trend-alone 12.7 %/1.19 - different window end (cache 09-16), and
this replay adds the band realism and charges the quoted spread on exits. All arms share the same replay, so the comparison holds.

**Verdict: the deployed trend sleeve stays as it is.** The multibagger hunt ends here: the "sentiment" names are momentum,
momentum is already in the book, and the ways of catching more of it (smaller names, longer holds, events) add drawdown faster
than return.
