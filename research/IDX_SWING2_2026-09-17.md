## Reading (written after the run; the verdicts above were fixed before it)

With the real closing spread the cost hurdle is lower than menu 1's tick model said: BLUE names 0.69 % a round trip
(0.30 % fees + 0.39 % spread), the 20-name basket 0.65 %, LIQ 0.86 %. It still eats everything at 2–5 days: no arm on any
universe is net positive with a t-stat above 2.5 at H <= 5; the random references lose 0.4–0.6 % a trade.

What has a real gross edge is 20 days and momentum-shaped: mom20|20 +1.47 % gross / +0.80 % net per trade (t 3.1),
brk20|20 +1.50 % / +0.86 %, brent_up|10 on energy names +1.92 % / +1.18 %. None is a candidate: mom20 loses in 2022 and 2026
(−21 %, −28 %) with a 51 % drawdown and a Sharpe of 0.26 — a momentum factor with crash risk, not a swing rule; brk20|20's
Sharpe 0.29 is not above random + 0.5; brent_up rides the sector's 2020–2026 drift (random energy names at H=10 already make
+0.55 % net). The operator's "ride the wave" arm — uptrend plus the desk's sentiment proxies (foreign net buying, trade-count
attention) — is WORSE than plain momentum (+0.09 % net at 20 days, −0.54 % at 10): the sentiment filters select the names
whose move is already over. Attention spikes (freq|3) are real (+0.70 % gross in 3 days) and fully consumed by the spread.
The split run-up is the one striking number (+7.3 % net per event, 77 % hit) on 22 events — a watch-list alert candidate, not
a strategy, until announcement dates are stored and the sample is ten times larger.

**Verdict: 46 trials over two menus (price, flow, order book, attention, events, macro, commodities, calendar, fundamentals),
0 candidates. Days-to-weeks trading on IDX does not survive its own spread with the data this desk holds. The question is
closed unless new data arrives (intraday prices, dated news sentiment, split announcement dates).**
