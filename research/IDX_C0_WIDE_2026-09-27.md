## Reading

RB-1 failed because a K-10 book samples ~10 % of a heavily skewed signal. Breadth fixes that: the result no longer depends on the
start date, the exits sit on a plateau, and costs x 2 hold. What breadth does NOT deliver is a return edge over the deployed
trend sleeve - the two earn about the same; the wide sleeve earns it with less risk and only half correlated. So the defensible
claim is diversification, not alpha: a second, lower-variance momentum sleeve. A forward test cannot resolve "better than REF"
(IR 0.12 needs decades); it can check "consistent with this backtest". Proposal: paper sleeve NEXT TO the trend sleeve, not instead.
