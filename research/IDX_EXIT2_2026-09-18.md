## Reading (written after the run; the reading rules above were fixed before it)

**A stop-loss on its own is not an exit for this book.** With only a stop, ten slots fill and never empty: 42 / 61 closed trades in
6.7 years — every one a loss, because the stop is the only door — and the equity is mostly ten names held to the last bar (+10 % /
+3 %/yr, mDD −41 / −53 %). A trend book needs a way out of its winners; the question is only which.

**Fixed brackets are worse than the trailing stop, and the tp3r corner is FRAGILE.** Percentage brackets (stop −10 %, target +20/30/50 %)
make +14..+21 %/yr on `small` against trail10's +29.9 %. The ATR bracket neighbourhood settles menu 13's open question: on `small` only
2 of 8 neighbours hold (a1.5x2, a1.5x4; a2.5x3 makes +30 % at mDD −46 %), on LIQ 5 of 8 — tp3r's +33.6 %/yr was a lucky corner of the
grid, not a better exit. Not proposed.

**Checking daily is worth about 4 %/yr.** The same 10 % trailing stop refreshed only weekly: +25.9 %/yr, Sharpe 1.18 (small); +15.4 %,
0.76 (LIQ). Refreshed monthly: +21.6 %/yr at mDD −41 % (small), +12.1 % (LIQ). The nightly chain does this refresh; the operator's
job is to act on the sell ticket the next morning.

**The one set-once hybrid that holds up: `run3r`** — stop at entry − 2 × ATR placed once; the 10 % trailing stop switches on only after
the name has made +3R (≈ +25–30 % on a typical small cap). small +33.3 %/yr, Sharpe 1.36, mDD −31 % (1 pp deeper than trail10);
LIQ +24.5 %/yr, Sharpe 1.08, mDD −34 % — BETTER than trail10 on LIQ by the declared bar, short of it on small (Sharpe +0.02, mDD −1 pp).
Practical merit: only the big winners need the daily refresh; everything else sits behind a stop that never moves. One arm of sixteen,
informative, not adopted — it would need its own neighbourhood (stop 1.5/2.5 × ATR, switch at 2R/4R) before any book changes.

**Verdict: 0 of 32 for money; 0 of 32 by the adoption rule; trail10 stays in both trend books. tp3r FRAGILE (closed). run3r
informative (LIQ only). The operator's practical answer: place the broker's stop-loss auto-order at entry − 10 % as a safety net —
it is exactly trail10's opening level — and let the nightly chain raise it; do not replace the trailing stop with a fixed one.**
